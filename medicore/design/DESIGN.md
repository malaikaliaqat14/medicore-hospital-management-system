## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-reliability clinical operating environment tailored for modern hospitals, outpatient facilities, and multi-specialty practices. Its ethos bridges surgical precision with empathetic, restorative calm—eliminating high-stress visual friction while upholding zero-error legibility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Clinically Reassuring:** Uses deep teal tones, crisp neutral surfaces, and ample breathing room to instill absolute confidence in patient data integrity and critical care workflows.
- **Systematic & Ergonomic:** Built for continuous 12-hour shifts. Reduces cognitive strain, visual clutter, and screen fatigue through soft tonal separation rather than jarring contrasts.
- **Modern Institutional Elegance:** Balances enterprise-grade durability with the refined finish of premier cloud software, rejecting legacy EHR clutter in favor of scannable, spatial clarity.

### Visual Style Direction
The aesthetic merges **Corporate / Modern SaaS architecture** with subtle **ambient-depth physical cues**. Functional panes, structured data grids, and floating telemetry readouts exist across well-defined surface tiers. Crisp 1px structural hairpins prevent boundary ambiguity in dense multiscreen setups, while soft atmospheric drop shadows establish fluid spatial depth for modals, slide-overs, and popovers.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a specialized clinical palette engineered for high-density legibility, strict WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility for interactive text, and rapid semantic identification across fast-paced environments.

### Core Brand Tokens
- **Primary Teal Base (`#0F766E`):** Represents institutional foundation, primary actions, and focused states.
- **Teal Vibrant Accent (`#14B8A6`):** Drives interactive active indicators, active triage selection, and key positive signals.
- **Teal Deep Midtone (`#0D9488`):** Serves hover actions and high-emphasis button states.
- **Info Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Drives telemetry alerts, non-urgent information feeds, and auxiliary clinical markers.

### Neutral Canvas System
- **App Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Cool off-white background mitigating glare during extended screen sessions.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity pure white surface for clinical metric groupings and patient records.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Defines clean component borders, tabular rules, and divided workspaces.
- **Typography Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate-black delivering maximum contrast without harsh pure-black eye fatigue.
- **Typography Secondary (`#475569`):** Balanced slate for clinical labels, timestamps, and secondary vitals metadata.
- **Typography Muted (`#94A3B8`):** Tertiary slate for disabled states, input watermarks, and subtle table gridlines.

### Clinical Workflow & Triage Semantic System
Each clinical status badge combines a distinct tinted background, structural border, and typography pairing:
- **`BOOKED`:** Slate/Sky tint (`bg: #F0F9FF`, `border: #BAE6FD`, `text: #0369A1`). Signifies upcoming scheduled appointments.
- **`CHECK_IN`:** Amber/Orange glow (`bg: #FFFBEB`, `border: #FDE68A`, `text: #B45309`). Denotes patient physically present in waiting bay.
- **`IN_PROGRESS`:** Emerald/Teal luminous tint (`bg: #CCFBF1`, `border: #5EEAD4`, `text: #0F766E`). Accompanied by a live pulse beacon indicator for ongoing consultations.
- **`COMPLETED`:** Subdued Herbal Green (`bg: #ECFDF5`, `border: #A7F3D0`, `text: #047857`). Signifies closed encounters, finalized charting, and generated invoices.
- **`CANCELLED` / `NO_SHOW`:** Neutral Muted Slate/Rose (`bg: #F1F5F9`, `border: #CBD5E1`, `text: #64748B`). Neutralizes voided slots without mimicking active warnings.
- **`CRITICAL_ALERT`:** Alert Red (`bg: #FEF2F2`, `border: #FECACA`, `text: #DC2626`). For critical lab values, anaphylaxis warnings, and code alerts.

### Dark Mode Semantic Mapping
- **Canvas Dark (`#090D16`):** Deep abyssal slate preventing contrast bloom in darkened wards.
- **Surface Dark Tier 1 (`#0F172A`):** Panel and sidebar base.
- **Surface Dark Tier 2 (`#1E293B`):** Elevated cards, prescription blocks, and modular trays.
- **Border Dark (`#334155`):** Structural division lines.
- **Text Dark Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp white-slate text ensuring effortless low-light reading.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is powered by **Inter**, leveraging its modern geometric construction, open apertures, and specialized OpenType tabular numerals for critical numerical display.

### Clinical Legibility Principles
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All numerical data—including vitals (BP, SpO2, Pulse), queue numbers, pharmaceutical dosages, and ICD-10 diagnostic codes—strictly enforce CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. This prevents layout shifts during live data feeds and aligns tabular ledgers perfectly.
- **Micro-Hierarchy in Charting:** Sub-labels, measurement units (mmHg, bpm, mg/dL), and dosage instructions employ `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to maintain legibility at glance speed.
- **Headline Rhythm:** Form headers, consultation sections, and patient identification ribbons use medium-to-bold weights with negative letter-spacing for clean, modern visual weight.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a rigid 8pt baseline spacing system (with a 4pt sub-increment for high-density clinical trays and status badges).

### Grid & Layout Strategy
- **Master Fluid Layout:** The desktop application operates on a 12-column adaptive grid spanning dynamic viewport widths with a max containment threshold of 1920px. 
- **Column System:** 
  - Desktop (>1280px): 12 columns, 24px (`gutter-desktop`) spacing, 32px (`margin-desktop`) outer padding.
  - Tablet (768px - 1279px): 8 columns, 20px (`gutter`) spacing, 24px (`margin`) outer padding.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4 columns, 12px (`gutter-mobile`) spacing, 16px (`margin-mobile`) outer padding.
- **Persistent Multi-Panel Frame:** Desktop views leverage an asymmetric layout:
  - Role-aware Sidebar: Fixed 260px expanded (collapsible to 72px icon rail).
  - Main Operational Stage: Fluid viewport accommodating split layouts (e.g., 60% charting workspace / 40% patient history & telemetry tray).
  - Quick Drawer: 420px slide-in panel reserved for prescriptions, lab order forms, and instant triage vitals entry.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through **tonal surface stacking** paired with **ambient, multi-tiered diffused shadowing**, deliberately avoiding heavy, murky borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `background-color: #F8FAFC`. Base backdrop across which all functional modules reside.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** `background-color: #FFFFFF`, hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for KPI summaries, patient summary cards, and calendar blocks.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Hover Cards):** Hairline border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for selected consultation entries, hovering scheduler slots, and dragging queue items.
- **Level 3 (Slide-in Panels & Menus):** `background-color: #FFFFFF`, hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to prescription builders, medication selectors, and popover menus.
- **Level 4 (System Modals & Critical Code Overlays):** Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`. Combined with a backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)`.

## Shapes
The design system adopts a **Level 2 (Rounded)** geometric geometry, using soft 8px–16px radii that offset clinical sterility with approachable, humane edges while maintaining high information density.

### Radius Application Guidelines
- **Core Components (`rounded` / 8px / 0.5rem):** Standard buttons, text inputs, search fields, table pagination controls, and nested vitals tiles.
- **Cards & Panes (`rounded-lg` / 16px / 1rem):** Primary clinic analytics cards, triage cards, active patient charts, calendar time-blocks, and appointment modals.
- **Clinical Flyouts & Overlays (`rounded-xl` / 24px / 1.5rem):** Large consultation slide-outs, inpatient ward layouts, and floating triage pads.
- **Micro-Badges & Status Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Clinical status badges (`BOOKED`, `CHECK_IN`, `IN_PROGRESS`), user tenant badges, and metric trend indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 8px, padding `10px 18px`. Hover: `#0D9488`. Active focus ring: `3px solid rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive/Emergency Action:** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#DC2626`. Focus ring: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`.

### Form Inputs & Clinical Data Entry
- **Standard Field:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, height 40px, padding `0 12px`, radius 8px.
- **Focused State:** Border `#0F766E`, outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`.
- **Telemetry / Vitals Input Group:** High-density split inputs with right-aligned units (e.g., `120/80` with right-anchored muted `mmHg` indicator in `label-sm`).

### Clinical Status Badges
- Engineered as full pill tokens (`rounded-full`, padding `3px 10px`, font `label-sm`, tracking `0.02em`).
- Always render with contextual contrast ratios exceeding 4.5:1. 
- The `IN_PROGRESS` token integrates an animated SVG pulse dot (4px circle in `#0D9488` with a 1.5s expanding aura).

### Patient Vitals & Clinical KPI Metric Cards
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius 16px, padding 20px.
- Top row: Semantic icon in a tinted roundel alongside category label (`label-md` in `#475569`).
- Core metric: `telemetry-vital` font with tabular figures.
- Delta footer: Trend badge showing directional micro-arrow with percentage change against baseline, colored by clinical trend (green for positive recovery markers, amber/red for acute deviations).

### Live Token Queue Board & Consultation Desk
- **Queue Row:** Draggable card with 8px radius, featuring prominent tabular Token ID (`#A-104`), patient avatar initials, arrival timestamp, and quick-action buttons (Call Next, Transfer Ward, Mark Complete).
- **Interactive Prescription Builder:** Multi-row ledger incorporating predictive drug autocomplete, strength selector chips (50mg, 100mg), frequency dropdowns (BID, TID, PRN), and automatic contraindication warning alerts rendered with amber left-side accent stripes.
